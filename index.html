About Echoes

Echoes tells you the stories of the places along your flight: cities, rivers, hills and the history beneath them. Once your stories are ready, everything is saved on your phone and works without internet.

How it knows where you are. With location on, it uses your phone's GPS. Without it, it follows the clock from take-off and says "around this part of your journey". It never promises you can see a place: cloud, haze, light and your seat all matter.

Checked stories. Stories on the Chandigarh → Bengaluru route are checked against the sources listed in each one. Others are marked as drafts until they're checked.

Maps are simplified and not for navigation.

How to play

Tap the sky or press Space to hop over the towers. When birds fly by, stay low and let them pass. The game speeds up as you go.

Pauses when you leave. Your flight keeps going in the background.
